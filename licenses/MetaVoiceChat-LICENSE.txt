ProximityVoiceChat's peer playback (Voice/Audio/PeerVoiceStream.cs) is derived from
MetaVoiceChat by Connor Myers (Metater): https://github.com/Metater/MetaVoiceChat

No MetaVoiceChat binaries ship with this mod. What was taken is the design of the
playback path, described in the header of that source file, plus a handful of Opus
encoder settings. MetaVoiceChat is MIT licensed and its notice follows.
